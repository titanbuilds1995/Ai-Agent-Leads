Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Who we are

Titan Realty Investments LLC ("Titan", "we") is a real estate investment company at 13075 Marcella Blvd, Loxahatchee, FL. We buy and resell residential property in South Florida and talk with listing agents and owners about specific properties.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us or that are publicly listed with a property for sale: name, brokerage, phone number, email address.
  • What you tell us about a property, and our conversation with you by email, phone or text.
  • When you submit our text message opt-in form: the details on the form, the date and time, your IP address and browser type, kept as the record of your consent.

How we use it

To talk with you about properties you have listed, offered or discussed with us, to make and follow up on offers, and to keep the records a real estate transaction needs.

Text messaging

We send text messages only to people who have agreed to receive them. Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, is never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Our SMS terms have the details.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us operate (email, telephone and text delivery, hosting, and the tools we use to track conversations), and only so they can provide that service to us. They may not use your information for their own purposes. We share information otherwise only when a transaction requires it (title, escrow, closing) or when the law requires it.

Keeping it

We keep conversation records for as long as we are active in the area and as long as the law requires for real estate transactions. Opt-out records are kept so we do not contact you again.

Your choices
