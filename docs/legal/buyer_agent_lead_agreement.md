## 1. Purpose
Titan finds and screens commercial investment properties for Buyer through its own software, data sources, outreach and relationships. This agreement sets out who does what on those properties so that Titan's work is protected and Agent's role with Buyer is respected.

## 2. Sourced Properties
A "Sourced Property" is any property, listing or opportunity that Titan first presents to Agent or Buyer in any form: by email, through Titan's deal website, in a written brief, on a call, in a message, or in any list or report Titan prepares. It includes the 92-unit property at ____________________________________ already presented, and every property Titan presents after the effective date.

A property Agent or Buyer can show they were already in active written discussions on before Titan presented it is not a Sourced Property, provided Agent says so in writing within three (3) business days of Titan's presentation.

## 3. Titan negotiates Sourced Properties
On every Sourced Property, Titan leads and conducts all contact and negotiation with the listing broker, the seller and the seller's representatives: book requests, questions, offers, counters, terms, and the path to contract. Agent and Buyer will not contact the listing broker or the seller about a Sourced Property directly, and will route any contact they receive to Titan the same day.

Buyer keeps every decision: whether to offer, at what price, and on what terms. Titan presents, Buyer decides.

## 4. Non-circumvention
For the term of this agreement and twenty-four (24) months after it ends, neither Agent nor Buyer, nor anyone acting for them or related to them, will pursue, offer on, contract for or acquire a Sourced Property, or any property owned by the same seller that Titan introduced, except through Titan under this agreement. This includes doing so through another entity, another agent, a family member or a partner.

## 5. Agent's role
Agent remains Buyer's representative for everything this agreement does not give to Titan: advising Buyer, the purchase documents, Buyer's exchange timeline, inspections, lenders, title and closing. Agent will share with Titan, promptly, anything that affects a Sourced Property (Buyer's decisions, proof of funds, timing, constraints).

## 6. Compensation
On any Sourced Property that Buyer or Buyer's affiliate acquires, Titan is paid ______________________________________________ at closing, from ______________________________. Agent's compensation from Buyer or from the transaction is Agent's own and is not reduced by this agreement unless the parties write otherwise here: ______________________________________.

## 7. Confidentiality
Titan's lists, screens, scores, brokers' responses, books received and the deal website are Titan's confidential work product. Agent and Buyer may use them only to evaluate Sourced Properties and will not share them with anyone outside Buyer's own team, lenders and advisors.

## 8. Term
This agreement runs for twelve (12) months from the effective date and renews month to month until either party ends it with thirty (30) days' written notice. Sections 4 (non-circumvention), 6 (compensation on Sourced Properties already presented) and 7 (confidentiality) survive the end of the agreement.

## 9. General
Florida law governs. Any dispute is heard in Palm Beach County, Florida. Each party will act in good faith and in writing where this agreement calls for notice. This is the whole agreement on its subject and changes only in a writing signed by both parties. Nothing here makes Titan a licensed broker for Buyer or makes Agent Titan's employee or partner.
