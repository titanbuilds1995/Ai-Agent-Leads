Text message (SMS) terms

Last updated October 26, 2026

Program

Titan Realty Investments LLC ("Titan", "we") sends conversational text messages to real estate agents and property owners about specific properties they have listed, offered or discussed with us: questions about a listing, seller flexibility, scheduling a walkthrough, and the status of an offer. Messages come from a member of our acquisitions team and are written for you, not broadcast.

How you opt in

We only text people who have agreed to it. You can agree by submitting our text message opt-in form, by replying to one of our emails or calls saying that texting is fine, or by texting our office line first. We keep a record of when and how you agreed.

Message frequency

Frequency varies with the conversation. A typical exchange about one property is a few messages over a couple of weeks. We do not send recurring alerts, newsletters or promotions by text.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Titan does not charge for text messages.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message and you will receive one confirmation text and nothing further. You can also email Charles@titanrealtyinvestments.com or call +1 (561) 405-7379 and ask us to stop. Opting out of texts does not affect email or phone contact unless you ask for that too.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, email Charles@titanrealtyinvestments.com or call +1 (561) 405-7379.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon and most other US carriers.

Your information

Mobile information collected for text messaging is never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our privacy policy.
